# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Project managers, logistics directors, civil/structural engineers, mining operators, and industrial contractors across South Africa who need mission-critical mobile crane hire, heavy lifting, rigging, and specialised abnormal transport.

## Product Purpose
The official corporate website and client portal for Teemane Heavy Haulage. It showcases full fleet capabilities (hydraulic cranes up to 440t, specialized trailers), safety track record, technical lift planning expertise, and drives immediate quote requests and 24/7/365 emergency dispatch.

## Positioning
Established in 2012 in Bellville South, Cape Town, Teemane combines 100+ years of collective management experience with a modern hydraulic crane fleet capable of lifts up to 440 tons. Available 24/7/365 for rapid-response heavy lifting, abnormal transport, and engineered CAD lift planning with zero-compromise safety standards.

## Operating Context
- Client site environments: Demanding civil construction sites, industrial plants, renewable energy installations, harbors, and remote mining operations across the Western Cape and South Africa.
- Operational urgency: Inquiries often require rapid turnaround, technical feasibility assessments, and 24-hour emergency mobilization.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Full fleet showcase: hydraulic mobile cranes (up to 440t), crane trucks, rigging equipment, multi-axle lowbeds, step decks, and abnormal transport trailers.
- Stack: Laravel 13 backend, Filament 5 CMS admin panel, React 19 + Inertia.js v3 frontend, Tailwind CSS v4, Shadcn UI primitives.
- Safety & compliance: Adherence to OHS regulations, certified riggers and crane operators, comprehensive method statements and lift engineering.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Teemane Heavy Haulage
- Voice: Authoritative, dependable, industrial powerhouse, precision-focused, unpretentious, and battle-tested.
- Visual anchor: High-visibility industrial contrasts, bold heavy-duty typography (Montserrat Black uppercase, tight tracking), functional clarity, real machinery and rigging operations photography on site.

## Evidence on Hand
- Authentic fleet and operation photography in `resources/images/heavy-haulage-*.webp`.
- Comprehensive service specifications in `data-docs/services/` and `resources/js/pages/services/`.
- Proven company history (est. 2012, 100+ years management experience, 440-ton capacity).

## Product Principles
1. **Unquestioned Capability**: Visuals and messaging immediately convey raw lifting capacity, engineering rigor, and heavy-duty authority.
2. **Speed to Mobilize**: Direct, friction-free paths to emergency contact (24/7/365) and detailed project quote estimation.
3. **Engineering & Safety First**: Emphasize meticulous lift planning, certified personnel, and flawless safety track records over superficial marketing fluff.
4. **Authenticity in Every Asset**: Real worksite photography and transparent equipment specs rather than generic stock imagery.
